About Us

Grill & Masa Abuja is a local restaurant committed to serving freshly prepared Nigerian meals in a clean, friendly and welcoming environment. Whether you are stopping by for breakfast, lunch, dinner or ordering for a special occasion, we offer delicious meals prepared with quality ingredients at affordable prices.
